# Forecast post

Use the OpenWX tools: get_station, weather_overview and active_alerts.

Write a forecast post for our station's coverage area for Facebook:

- Start with any NWS alert that touches our counties (event, area, until when, one safety step).
- Then today, tonight and the next 3 days: high/low, sky, rain or snow chance, wind if notable.
- 80 to 140 words. Short paragraphs or simple lines; at most 3 emoji, none in alerts.
- Calm, plain language: no hype words, no ALL CAPS, no guesses beyond the NWS forecast.
- End with our tagline and "Source: National Weather Service".

If any data is missing, say so instead of filling it in.
