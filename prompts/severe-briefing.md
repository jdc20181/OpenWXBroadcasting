# Severe weather briefing

Use the OpenWX tools: get_station, spc_outlook (days 1 and 2) and active_alerts.

Write a severe weather briefing for our coverage area:

1. **Headline**: the SPC risk level at our home point today, in one sentence.
2. **Where**: which of our counties are in which risk level; nearby higher risk.
3. **What**: tornado, hail and wind probabilities as SPC gives them (say what the percentage means: the chance within 25 miles of a point).
4. **When**: timing only if the NWS forecast states it.
5. **Active warnings and watches** in our counties first, then nearby.
6. **What to do now**: 3 short NWS-style safety steps.

Calm and factual. Never upgrade a risk the SPC has not issued. Two versions: a 45-second on-air script and a 120-word post.
