# Station setup by conversation

Help me set up OpenWX for my station. Call get_station first, then ask me, one question at a time, only for what is missing:

1. The station name as it should appear on air, and a short name.
2. A contact e-mail or website for the National Weather Service (it is sent with data requests, not published).
3. My state.
4. The counties I cover.
5. My main city or town (the map centres there).
6. A name for the coverage area, like "Southern Indiana".

Then call set_station_identity and set_station_region, show me the result, and remind me to restart OpenWX Broadcasting.
