# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want OpenWX to be a welcoming project for everyone who cares about weather and their
community: broadcasters, volunteers, students, emergency managers and hobbyists, whatever their
experience, background or identity. We pledge to make taking part a harassment-free experience
for everyone.

## Our standards

Good conduct includes:

- being kind and patient, especially with people new to weather, broadcasting or code;
- giving and taking feedback on the work, not the person;
- assuming good intent, and asking before assuming;
- crediting other people's work, data sources and ideas;
- owning mistakes and learning from them.

Not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks;
- sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention;
- publishing someone's private information (home address, e-mail, a station's credentials)
  without permission;
- spreading weather misinformation on purpose or using the project to create panic;
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chats) and when you
represent the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub (a private security advisory works
for anything sensitive) or by asking in an issue for a private contact. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and warn, suspend or ban people whose
conduct breaks this code, in proportion to what happened:

1. **Correction**: a private note explaining what was wrong.
2. **Warning**: a recorded warning with consequences for repeat behaviour.
3. **Temporary ban** from project spaces.
4. **Permanent ban** for sustained or severe violations.
